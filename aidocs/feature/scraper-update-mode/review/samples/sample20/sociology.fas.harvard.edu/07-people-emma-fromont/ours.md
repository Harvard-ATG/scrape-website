# Emma Fromont

**Research Interests: **Immigration, Ethnicity, Race, and Nationalism, Qualitative Methods, Identity Formation, Racial Formation

**Emma Fromont** is a PhD student in Sociology at Harvard University. She is interested in the articulation of national, ethnic, and racial identities in migration and in shifting ethno-racial boundaries across national borders. She specifically studies contemporary French Antillean migrants in Canada, as well as elite Moroccan migrants in France, examining how class and citizenship status shape racial and ethnic boundary-making.
