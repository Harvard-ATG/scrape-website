 

#  Douglas Marshall (PhD 2011) accepts tenure-track position at Carleton College 

 





February 28, 2014

 

 

Doug Marshall, currently a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Minnesota Center for Philosophy of Science, has accepted a tenure-track position in the Department of Philosophy at Carleton College.

Marshall's research focuses on the philosophy of mathematics and the natural sciences, as well as on early modern philosophy.
