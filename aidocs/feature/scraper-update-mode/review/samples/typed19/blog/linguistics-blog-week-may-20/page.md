 

#  Week of May 20 

 





May 21, 2019

 

 

###  Harvard at CLS 55

 The following talks featured Harvard presenters at the [55th annual Chicago Linguistics Society](http://chicagolinguisticsociety.org/conference.html) conference held at the University of Chicago on May 16-19, 2019

- [Shannon Bryant](/people/shannon-bryant): "Effects of creator role on binding into Picture Noun Phrases" (joint work with [Isabelle Charnavel](/people/isabelle-charnavel))
- Deniz Satik: "Logophoric Pronouns are not Inherently Logophoric"
- [Yingtong Liu](/people/yingtong-liu): "Factive and manner-of-speaking islands are an artifact of nonlinearity in the acceptability judgement task" (poster; joint work with [Rachel Ryskin](http://web.mit.edu/ryskin/www/) (MIT), [Richard Futrell](http://socsci.uci.edu/~rfutrell/) (UC Irvine), and [Edward Gibson](https://bcs.mit.edu/users/egibsonmitedu) (MIT))
