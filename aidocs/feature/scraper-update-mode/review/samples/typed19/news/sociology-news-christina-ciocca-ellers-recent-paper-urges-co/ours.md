# Christina Ciocca Eller's recent paper urges college ratings focused on real-life outcomes

[Harvard Gazette](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2024/02/are-top-ranked-colleges-really-the-best-depends-at-what/?utm_source=SilverpopMailing&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=Daily%20Gazette%2020240214%20(1))discusses

[Christina Ciocca Eller](https://sociology.fas.harvard.edu/people/christina-ciocca-eller)'s research in a paper published this month in the

[Journal of American Sociology](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/full/10.1086/727891). Ciocca Eller calls for new government and media ratings that measure how U.S. institutions of higher education shape the outcomes of those they enroll.
